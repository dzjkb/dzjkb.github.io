_China's quest to engineer the future_
main thesis - US is run by lawyers, China by engineers
I really don't think that's accurate, should be interesting nonetheless

**update 1/3 in**: yep it got interesting, nice

**just finished update**:
loved it! great overview of China's rise as an industrial superpower and it's various strengths and weaknesses, and an overall comparison of the US and China along different axes

the main thesis (lawyers vs engineers) popped up constantly in random assertions about "proceduralism" and characterizations of America's left and right, but it wasn't something that he was trying to prove, rather a figure of speech to logically encapsulate his framework which does make _some_ sense imo, although I think he was going too far with the matter-of-fact reductions

definitely gave me a way better view into what China is actually about
