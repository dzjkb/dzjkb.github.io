A richer flow (autoregressive, permutations, whatever) would do all of that better — tighter Gaussian match, more dimension-mixing. But none of that is the same thing as "better organization" in the sense you care about, and here's why it's a trap:

  A posterior flow is a bijection trained to match the prior — it has no incentive to organize anything perceptually. Its loss is KL(q ‖ N(0,I)).
